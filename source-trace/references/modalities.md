# Handling each modality

## Text and web pages
- Record the URL and the access date; pages change.
- Follow links from secondary coverage to the primary source when one exists.

## PDFs
- Cite page numbers, and the table or figure number for data.
- Check numbers against tables, not against the abstract.

## Images
- Describe what the image shows before judging what it is claimed to show.
- Look for earlier copies of the image to establish its origin.

## Audio and video
- Cite timestamps (`mm:ss`) for every quoted statement.
- Work from a transcript when one is available, and spot-check it against the recording.
